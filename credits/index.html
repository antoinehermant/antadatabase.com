Credits

The isochrones are those of the datasets of the database, each credited with its citation in the viewer and in the catalogue. The website and the 3D viewer also use the work below.

Data in the 3D viewer

  • Bed and surface topography: Bedmap3 (Pritchard et al.), CC BY 4.0.
  • Survey lines: BEDMAP1, 2 and 3 (Frémand et al., 2023), CC BY 4.0.
  • Grounding lines: RAISED (Bentley et al., 2014).
  • Ice divides: IMBIE2 drainage basins (Rignot et al.).
  • Ice velocity: MEaSUREs InSAR-Based Antarctica Ice Velocity Map, version 2 (Rignot et al., NSIDC-0484).
